Department Aptitude Test
Student privacy and data use

This test handles a student's name, student ID, class, department, answers, score and test timing to administer assessments and keep results.

In this offline app, this information is saved in the browser's storage on this device; the app does not upload it to a school server. Teachers can export results to CSV. Anyone with access to this browser profile or an exported file may be able to see the data.

Results remain in this browser until a teacher clears them or browser storage is removed. Exported CSV files are separate and must be securely stored and deleted by the person who exported them.

Students or their parents/guardians should direct requests to access, correct or delete data to the school's designated privacy contact. Before using this with students, the school must publish that contact, its lawful basis, retention period and the applicable student/parent rights, and determine whether any additional parent/guardian steps are required.

This notice is an operational disclosure, not legal advice or a substitute for the school's approved privacy policy.
